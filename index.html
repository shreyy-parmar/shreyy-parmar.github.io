Cloud-native engineer, governance-minded builder

I'm an Azure Data Engineer with over 2 years of experience designing, developing, and optimizing data pipelines and cloud-based architectures using Microsoft Azure and open-source tooling. I work across the full stack — ingesting from APIs, on-prem SQL, and cloud storage with Azure Data Factory and Synapse Pipelines, transforming at scale with PySpark on Databricks, and landing curated data in Synapse Analytics and Snowflake warehouses.

Medallion architecture — Bronze, Silver, Gold — is the backbone of how I structure pipelines: raw data in, validated and modeled in the middle, business-ready on the way out. I care as much about data governance as I do about throughput — RBAC, ACLs, managed identities, and Purview lineage are part of how I build, not an afterthought.

Currently based in Toronto, Canada, with a Master's in Computer Science from Lakehead University and a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering from Gujarat Technological University, India.
